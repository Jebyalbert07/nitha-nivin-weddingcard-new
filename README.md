## ✨ Key Features

- **Interactive Monogrammed Envelope:** Wax seal click experience with confetti and welcome greeting.
- **Embedded Ambient Music:** Romantic acoustic piano & harp chord progression using the Web Audio API with a floating toggle.
- **Real-Time Countdown:** Live countdown timers for both the Wedding Day and Engagement Ceremony.
- **Botanical Eucalyptus & Floral Accents:** Watercolor green and golden motifs matching the printed card.
- **Google Calendar & `.ics` Downloads:** One-click calendar sync for Apple, Google, and Outlook calendars.
- **Venues & Google Maps Navigation:** Direct Google Maps navigation links and landmark guidelines for traveling guests.
- **Dress Code Guide:** Aesthetic color palette swatches (Olive Green, Sage Mist, Champagne Gold, Warm Ivory).
- **Interactive RSVP System:** Instant RSVP submission with confetti celebration, localStorage persistence, and direct WhatsApp RSVP forwarding to the family.
- **Live Guestbook:** Guests can post congratulatory notes, prayers, and like other blessings.
- **Wedding Moments & Memories Gallery:** Dedicated full gallery with category filter tabs (*All, Portraits, Pre-Wedding, Engagement, Candids*), interactive likes, fullscreen lightbox with next/prev navigation, and guest photo contribution upload.
- **Original Wedding Card Lightbox:** High-resolution zoom, rotate, and image download feature for the physical card.
- **Family Contact Helpline:** Direct phone and WhatsApp links for both families.
